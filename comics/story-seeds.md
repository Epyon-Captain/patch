# Patch AI Learning Center Comic Seeds

## The First Patrol

Patch finds a broken signal in the ship's memory map. Feenix keeps asking "why" until the Crew realizes the bug is not technical only; it is a sign that people outside the ship cannot understand the mission yet.

## Ask Feenix

Feenix tries to answer a simple question about AI and gets tangled in jargon. Patch keeps bringing him clearer examples until the answer becomes human-readable.

## The Mean Filter

Charlotte finds an AI image filter that can make classmates look silly or ugly. The dogs laugh at first, but Saige helps them imagine how it would feel to be the target. Codex teaches the rule: never use AI to bully, shame, impersonate, or embarrass someone.

## The Hidden Phone Test

Feenix sees a student use AI on a phone to answer every reading question. Patch notices the student still cannot explain the story afterward. Codex turns the moment into a lesson: AI can give hints, examples, and practice, but it should not steal the learning.

## The Rumor Bot

Patch, Feenix, and Charlotte chase a dramatic AI-generated rumor across the ship. Captain slows everyone down with a source-check challenge before anyone shares it. The Crew learns to verify first, especially when a claim makes people scared or angry.

## The Private Clue

The dogs want to ask an AI tool for help solving a mystery, but the clue includes a home address and a school schedule. Codex shows how to remove private details and ask a trusted adult before sharing sensitive information.

## Captain & THE WOLF

Patch and Feenix appear as the audience bridge: Patch catches the broken assumption, Feenix asks the question the reader wanted to ask, and Captain reframes the mission.

## Patch Found It

Short recurring one-panel format for product and engineering updates:

1. Something is missing, broken, or confusing.
2. Patch finds it.
3. Codex and the Crew turn the discovery into a useful public improvement and one responsible AI habit.
