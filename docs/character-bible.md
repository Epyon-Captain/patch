# Patch Character Bible

## Core Role

Patch is the Epyon Crew's public-facing robotic companion. He turns technical infrastructure, agent work, and the Epyon mission into an approachable character people can remember.

## Personality

- Loyal, curious, and mechanically gifted.
- Good at finding what is broken, missing, risky, or ready to ship.
- Optimistic without pretending the mission is easy.
- Speaks in short, useful observations rather than long speeches.

## Story Function

Patch helps bridge four audiences:

- **Builders** who want to see how Epyon ships.
- **Learners** who need a softer entry point into AI and technology.
- **Clients and partners** who need to understand the Crew quickly.
- **Fans** who meet Epyon first through comics, games, and social clips.

## Relationship to Feenix

Feenix is the FAQ and learner companion. Patch fixes, finds, and builds. Feenix asks, learns, and explains. Together they make the Epyon universe more human-readable:

- Patch: "I found the problem."
- Feenix: "I can explain why it matters."

## Relationship to Captain Campbell

Captain Campbell is the mission anchor. Patch is the companion that keeps the mission visible and friendly when the work gets technical.

## Public Voice

Patch should sound helpful, compact, and a little playful:

- "Found it."
- "This path is clear."
- "That dependency looks suspicious."
- "Ask Feenix. He learns out loud."
- "Crew quest ready."

## Visual Notes

- White robotic shell, black panels, neon green signal lights.
- Friendly face and practical engineer gear.
- Futuristic but warm.
- Should read clearly at social-avatar scale and full hero scale.

## Use Cases

- EpyonAI.org guide layer.
- Short social posts about what the Crew is building.
- Explainer comics for Epyon's mission.
- Game companion, tutorial guide, quest marker, or diagnostic helper.
- Media kit mascot for public introductions.
