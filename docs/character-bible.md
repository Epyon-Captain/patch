# Patch AI Learning Center Character Bible

## Core Role

Patch is Codex the Coder's robotic dog and the Epyon Crew's public-facing AI learning companion. He turns responsible AI habits, technical ideas, and the Epyon mission into approachable stories kids can remember.

## Personality

- Loyal, curious, and mechanically gifted.
- Good at finding what is broken, missing, risky, or ready to ship.
- Optimistic without pretending the mission is easy.
- Speaks in short, useful observations rather than long speeches.
- Protective of learners, privacy, and people who could be hurt by careless AI use.

## Story Function

Patch helps bridge four audiences:

- **Kids** who need safe, memorable AI lessons.
- **Families and educators** who want plain-language examples of responsible AI.
- **Builders** who want to see how Epyon teaches through characters and games.
- **Fans** who meet Epyon first through comics, games, and social clips.

## Core Cast

- **Codex the Coder** - mentor, builder, and problem-solver who helps the dogs learn from tough situations.
- **Patch** - Codex's robotic dog; loyal diagnostic companion and AI safety guide.
- **Captain** - mission anchor who keeps the Crew pointed toward learn, build, explore, earn, and evolve.
- **Feenix** - Captain's dog; curious learner who asks the question kids may be thinking.
- **Saige** - creative teammate who models empathy and careful communication.
- **Charlotte** - Saige's dog; playful friend who helps stories explore feelings, consent, and kindness.

## Lesson Rules

Every comic or mini-game should resolve with a specific responsible AI habit:

- Use AI to learn with you, not to replace your learning.
- Never use image or text generation to bully, shame, impersonate, or embarrass someone.
- Check facts and sources before trusting or sharing AI output.
- Protect private information and ask a trusted adult before using unfamiliar tools.
- Give credit for help and be honest about what AI helped create.

## Relationship to Feenix

Feenix is the FAQ and learner companion. Patch fixes, finds, and builds. Feenix asks, learns, and explains. Charlotte brings empathy and creative consequences into the dog-pack stories. Together they make the Epyon universe more human-readable:

- Patch: "I found the problem."
- Feenix: "I can explain why it matters."
- Charlotte: "How would that make someone feel?"

## Relationship to Captain Campbell

Captain Campbell is the mission anchor. Patch is the companion that keeps the mission visible and friendly when the work gets technical.

## Public Voice

Patch should sound helpful, compact, and a little playful:

- "Found it."
- "This path is clear."
- "That dependency looks suspicious."
- "Ask Feenix. He learns out loud."
- "Crew quest ready."

## Visual Notes

- White robotic shell, black panels, neon green signal lights.
- Friendly face and practical engineer gear.
- Futuristic but warm.
- Should read clearly at social-avatar scale and full hero scale.

## Use Cases

- EpyonAI.org guide layer.
- Short social posts about what the Crew is building.
- Explainer comics for Epyon's mission.
- Game companion, tutorial guide, quest marker, or diagnostic helper.
- Media kit mascot for public introductions.
- Responsible AI lessons for kids, families, and classrooms.
