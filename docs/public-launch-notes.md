## Public-Safe Content Rules

- Keep all copy kid-friendly and public-safe.
- Do not publish credentials, private logs, browser data, internal recovery dumps, or local `.env` files.
- Do not include private source paths in public-facing copy unless they are intentionally published documentation paths.
- Avoid stories that teach harmful tactics in detail; focus on consequences, repair, and safer choices.
- Make every comic or game connect back to learn, build, explore, earn, and evolve.
