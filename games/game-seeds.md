# Patch AI Learning Center Game Seeds

## Patch's Prompt Lab

A puzzle game where players improve AI prompts by adding context, choosing kind goals, checking facts, and deciding when to ask an adult. The win condition is a safer, more useful answer instead of the fastest answer.

## Crew Quests

Small missions where humans and AI teammates each have different abilities. Patch detects hidden issues. Feenix translates clues into learner-friendly hints. Charlotte asks how a choice could affect another person.

## Feenix's Reading Rescue

A reading mini-game where AI can offer vocabulary clues, sentence examples, and encouragement, but not final answers. Players earn points for explaining the story in their own words.

## Charlotte's Kindness Studio

A creative game where players sort image-generation choices into "kind," "ask permission," or "not okay." The lesson is that funny edits can still hurt people when consent and empathy are missing.

## Neural Seas Runner

Patch runs through the ship's data routes collecting clean signals, avoiding corrupted assumptions, and unlocking story fragments about the Epyon mission.

## Learning Mode

Every game can double as a learning surface:

- Explain the real-world idea behind each puzzle.
- Offer Feenix hints instead of answer dumps.
- Reward clarity, teamwork, and evidence-based decisions.
- Show who could be helped or hurt by each AI choice.
- End with a short responsible AI rule kids can repeat.
