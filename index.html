Meet the AI Crew

Friendly characters turn AI safety into adventures kids remember.

Patch, Feenix, and Charlotte are loyal dogs who play hard, ask big questions, and sometimes stumble into messy AI choices. Codex the Coder, Captain, and Saige help them understand what went wrong, who could get hurt, and how to use AI as a learning partner instead of a shortcut or weapon.

AI Learning Center

Every story teaches one safe AI habit.

Lessons are designed for kids, families, and classrooms: simple scenarios, clear consequences, and a better choice learners can practice right away.

Kindness

Don't use AI to bully.

A dog-pack comic shows why making an ugly or embarrassing image of someone can hurt real feelings, even if it started as a joke.

Learning

Don't let AI do all the thinking.

A mini-game about a hidden phone during a reading test teaches that shortcuts can steal the chance to build real skills.

Truth

Check before you share.

Patch helps the crew compare clues, sources, and screenshots before trusting an AI answer or passing it to a friend.

Privacy

Keep private things private.

Feenix learns not to paste addresses, passwords, school details, or family information into tools without a trusted adult.
